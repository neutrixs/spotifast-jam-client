Spotifast with jams: listen together with friends, each on your own Spotify account. Built on Spotifast 0.17.1.

**Download:** [Windows](https://github.com/neutrixs/spotifast-jam-client/releases/download/v0.17.101/spotifast-v0.17.101-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc.msi) · [Windows ARM](https://github.com/neutrixs/spotifast-jam-client/releases/download/v0.17.101/spotifast-v0.17.101-aarch64-pc-windows-msvc.msi) · [Linux](https://github.com/neutrixs/spotifast-jam-client/releases/download/v0.17.101/spotifast-v0.17.101-x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu.tar.gz) · [AppImage](https://github.com/neutrixs/spotifast-jam-client/releases/download/v0.17.101/spotifast-0.17.101-x86_64.AppImage)

Installing it on Windows replaces Spotifast if you already have it, keeping your settings and sign-in. Windows may warn that the installer is from an unknown publisher: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New

- **Jams.** Click the speaker button, choose **Start a jam** and enter a jam server's address; the invite link is copied for you. Friends open the link and Spotifast joins the jam.
- **Everyone follows the host's song,** at the host's place, with the host's shuffle and repeat.
- **Everyone has the same controls:** play, pause, seek, skip, shuffle, repeat, and starting any song, album or playlist.
- **One shared queue.** Anyone adds songs, puts them next, reorders them, removes them or plays one now, from the jam block at the top of the queue.

Updates now come from this fork's releases, so a newer Spotifast without jams is never installed over this one.
